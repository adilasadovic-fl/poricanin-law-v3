About us · Our founder

Our founder and almost 20 years advising employers

Poricanin Law was founded by Emina Poricanin, a labor and employment attorney and technology entrepreneur. Before founding the firm she was an equity partner in the Labor and Employment division of an AmLaw 200 international law firm.

Having built and run her own companies, she approaches legal problems the way business owners do: weighing risk against growth, cost, operations and leadership priorities, and recommending a course of action the business can execute.

Our firm

We provide businesses with ongoing, practical legal counsel across every part of their operations.

Many of our clients rely on us as their general counsel. We advise on corporate operations, employment, compliance, transactions, litigation, risk management and strategic business decisions, building relationships that last for years rather than single matters.

The firm has represented hundreds of employers and provided strategic counsel on thousands of employment-related matters, helping organizations minimize risk, maintain compliance and achieve their business objectives. We are based in New York, and our clients operate across the United States and internationally.
