Labor & Employment Law

Management-side counsel on federal and state labor and employment law, for employers of every size.

7 areas
  • Employment law compliance

    Counsel on the Family and Medical Leave Act, New York Paid Family Leave Law, New York City Safe and Sick Leave Law, New York Human Rights Law, the Americans with Disabilities Act and New York Labor Law.

  • Wage & hour compliance

    Federal and state wage and hour laws, payment practices, overtime exemption and worker classification, and wage parity requirements for New York home care employers.

  • Union relations & collective bargaining

    National Labor Relations Act compliance, union avoidance, responding to organizing campaigns, and negotiating first and successor collective bargaining agreements, including as lead negotiator at the table.

  • Discrimination, harassment & retaliation

    Counsel on discrimination laws and defense of employers against claims of discrimination, harassment and retaliation.

  • Hiring, discipline & separations

    Background checks and FCRA disclosures, ban-the-box laws, drug testing, discipline and discharge, termination counseling, reductions in force, severance and separation agreements that comply with the OWBPA.

  • Employment agreements & restrictive covenants

    Executive employment contracts, noncompetition and non-solicitation covenants, agreements for multi-jurisdictional employees, and enforcement against departing employees.

  • Unemployment insurance & workers’ compensation

    Advice on unemployment insurance claims and disputes, and on workers’ compensation matters.

Workplace Advisory & Risk Management

Preventive, hands-on support that keeps workplace issues from becoming claims.

7 areas
  • Policies & handbooks

    Workplace policies and employee handbooks, including policies that align leave rules across multi-state workforces.

  • Workplace investigations

    Internal investigations of workplace misconduct, including harassment, discrimination and whistleblower allegations, and coordination with government and law enforcement where needed.

  • Leave & accommodation management

    Guiding employers through the interactive process for employees with disabilities, intermittent FMLA leave and attendance documentation.

  • Independent contractors & gig workers

    Agreements and policies that support independent contractor status, and oversight of contractor relationships.

  • Management & employee training

    Training programs, including:

    • Wage and hour compliance for managers
    • Employee leave laws for administrative and executive staff
    • Responding to union organizing
    • Preventing harassment and discrimination
    • Best hiring practices
    • Protecting trade secrets and confidential information
  • Employee relations & culture

    Culture surveys and assessments, support through rapid growth or a merger, open-door and reporting channels, and advice to boards on workplace culture.

  • Outsourced HR counsel

    Day-to-day human resources support from the firm’s attorneys and consultants for organizations without a full in-house HR function.
