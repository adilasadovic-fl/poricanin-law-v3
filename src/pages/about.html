About us · Our founder

Our founder and almost 20 years advising employers

Poricanin Law was founded by Emina Poricanin, a labor and employment attorney and technology entrepreneur. Before founding the firm she was an equity partner in the Labor and Employment division of an AmLaw 200 international law firm.

Having built and run her own companies, she approaches legal problems the way business owners do: weighing risk against growth, cost, operations and leadership priorities, and recommending a course of action the business can execute.
