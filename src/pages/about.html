Emina Poricanin

Founder, labor and employment attorney

Read our insights

Emina Poricanin has advised employers for almost 20 years. Before founding Poricanin Law, she was an equity partner in the Labor and Employment division of an AmLaw 200 international law firm.

She began her legal career as a court attorney at the New York Appellate Division, Fourth Department, and today leads a team of attorneys and consultants advising business owners, executives and boards.

Emina is also the founder of a payroll technology company focused on wage and hour compliance. Running a business of her own shapes how the firm works: legal risk is weighed against cost, growth and operations, and advice comes as a course of action the business can carry out.

She writes and speaks regularly on labor and employment law. She is the author of The Employment Law Manual for New York Employers, hosts the Home Care Forum podcast, and presents at industry conferences and the firm's webinars.
