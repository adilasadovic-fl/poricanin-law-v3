Practice areas

What we do for

your business

From ongoing outside general counsel to audits, disputes and transactions, we advise business owners, executives and boards on the legal questions that come with running and growing a company.

What the firm does

Outside General Counsel & Business Advisory

Ongoing counsel for owners, executives and boards on day-to-day operational challenges, corporate governance, contract drafting and negotiation, and strategic decisions. We serve as chief legal counsel for organizations that need an experienced advisor without an in-house legal department.

Labor & Employment Law

Management-side counsel on federal and state employment law, including wage and hour, leave and accommodation, discrimination and harassment, and union relations and collective bargaining. Representation before the EEOC, NLRB, Division of Human Rights and Departments of Labor.

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

Defense of businesses in commercial and employment disputes, including class action wage and hour litigation in federal and state court and grievance arbitrations. Our focus is a resolution that makes business sense, whether that is a negotiated settlement or a defended case.

Regulatory Compliance & Government Investigations

Representation in government audits and investigations, including Department of Health, Office of Medicaid Inspector General, Attorney General and Department of Labor matters. We also handle licensing and compliance programs, plus Medicare and Medicaid requirements for healthcare providers.

Corporate Transactions & Commercial Contracts

Business formation and restructuring, acquisitions and sales, due diligence and joint ventures, with particular depth in regulated healthcare transactions. We also negotiate vendor, commercial and executive employment contracts.

Workplace Advisory & Risk Management

Preventive, hands-on support that keeps workplace issues from becoming claims: handbooks and policies, independent contractor classification, internal investigations and manager training. We also run privileged self-audits that find and fix problems before an agency does.

Not sure which area fits? Tell us about the issue and we will point you in the right direction.
