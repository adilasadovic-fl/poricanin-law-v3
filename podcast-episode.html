When your EVV vendor also runs your payroll

Episode notes

Combining scheduling, EVV, billing and payroll with one vendor can look efficient. In this episode Emina explains why, for New York home care agencies, it can also hand the State the evidence of wage parity violations.

Under New York’s Wage Parity Law, no Medicaid payment may be made for care furnished by an aide who was paid below the required total compensation, and providers certify compliance with every claim. When that certification is wrong, the exposure moves from a payroll error to the federal and New York False Claims Acts, with treble damages and a penalty for each claim.

Emina walks through recent settlements, the questions an integrated EVV and payroll vendor cannot easily answer, and what upstate agencies outside the Wage Parity Law should still watch for.

Mentioned in this episode

  • The Criminal and Civil Exposure When Your EVV and Billing Company Also Becomes Your Payroll Vendor
  • New York Public Health Law § 3614-c (the Wage Parity Law)
  • The federal and New York False Claims Acts
