Practice area

Outside General Counsel & Business Advisory

Ongoing counsel for owners, executives and boards, from a company’s formation to its dissolution.

6 areas
  • Outside general counsel

    We serve as chief legal counsel for client organizations, advising on daily operational challenges, human resources and personnel issues, corporate governance, contracts, disputes and any other legal matter that arises.

    Emina has served as general counsel to the New York State Association of Health Care Providers (HCP).
  • Corporate governance & board advisory

    Advice to owners, boards of directors and trustees on governance requirements, the fiduciary obligations of directors, self-disclosure and compliance matters, and board training.

  • Business formation & market entry

    Choosing the right business structure, registering with state and federal authorities, and helping entrepreneurs and new entrants identify markets and opportunities, obtain licensure and navigate regulatory approval.

  • Government relations & legislative advocacy

    Advocacy and government relations on legislative and regulatory issues that affect clients’ markets and operations.

  • Crisis resolution

    Counsel when a business faces an urgent legal, regulatory or operational crisis, including government action that threatens its ability to operate.

    Often for hospitality, manufacturing and education clients.
  • Immigration

    Immigration-related requirements for new hires and business immigration matters for client organizations.

    Often for manufacturing and hospitality clients.
Practice area

Labor & Employment Law

Management-side counsel on federal and state labor and employment law, for employers of every size.

7 areas
  • Employment law compliance

    Counsel on the Family and Medical Leave Act, New York Paid Family Leave Law, New York City Safe and Sick Leave Law, New York Human Rights Law, the Americans with Disabilities Act and New York Labor Law.

  • Wage & hour compliance

    Federal and state wage and hour laws, payment practices, overtime exemption and worker classification, and wage parity requirements for New York home care employers.

  • Union relations & collective bargaining

    National Labor Relations Act compliance, union avoidance, responding to organizing campaigns, and negotiating first and successor collective bargaining agreements, including as lead negotiator at the table.

  • Discrimination, harassment & retaliation

    Counsel on discrimination laws and defense of employers against claims of discrimination, harassment and retaliation.

  • Hiring, discipline & separations

    Background checks and FCRA disclosures, ban-the-box laws, drug testing, discipline and discharge, termination counseling, reductions in force, severance and separation agreements that comply with the OWBPA.

  • Employment agreements & restrictive covenants

    Executive employment contracts, noncompetition and non-solicitation covenants, agreements for multi-jurisdictional employees, and enforcement against departing employees.

  • Unemployment insurance & workers’ compensation

    Advice on unemployment insurance claims and disputes, and on workers’ compensation matters.

Practice area

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

Representation of businesses in court, in arbitration and before government agencies.

6 areas
  • Commercial disputes

    Representation of businesses in commercial disputes, with a focus on outcomes that make business sense.

  • Employment litigation & class actions

    Defense of employers in employment disputes, including class action wage and hour litigation in federal and state court.

  • Grievance arbitration

    Grievance arbitrations under collective bargaining agreements, for private employers and educational institutions.

  • Agency proceedings

    Representation of employers before the New York State Division of Human Rights, the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, the National Labor Relations Board, the Public Employment Relations Board, and federal and state Departments of Labor.

  • Payor & managed care disputes

    Disputes between healthcare providers and payors, including managed care organizations.

    Often for healthcare and home care clients.
  • Healthcare litigation

    Healthcare litigation up to and including bet-the-company cases, such as the multi-year litigation over compensation for aides working 24-hour shifts.

Practice area

Regulatory Compliance & Government Investigations

Audits, investigations and compliance programs, with deep experience in Medicaid-funded and highly regulated businesses.

9 areas
  • Government audits & investigations

    Representation in audits and investigations before the New York Attorney General, the Office of Medicaid Inspector General, the Labor Bureau, the New York City Department of Consumer Affairs, the New York Department of Health, and the New York and U.S. Departments of Labor.

  • OSHA & workplace safety audits

    Representation in audits and investigations under the Occupational Safety and Health Act.

    Often for manufacturing clients.
  • Compliance programs & outside compliance officer

    Designing and implementing corporate compliance programs, internal audit and monitoring, risk assessments and corrective measures, and serving as an outside corporate compliance officer.

  • Privileged self-audits

    Self-audits conducted under attorney-client privilege to find and correct noncompliance before a government audit begins.

  • Licensing, certification & accreditation

    Initial licensing and accreditation before federal and state regulators, satisfying licensing conditions, and defending applications before approval boards.

    Often for healthcare and home care providers.
  • Medicare, Medicaid & reimbursement

    Conditions of participation, Medicaid and Medicare program requirements, reimbursement issues and appeals.

    Often for healthcare and home care providers.
  • HIPAA & privacy

    HIPAA and privacy compliance, including management training.

    Often for healthcare providers.
  • FOIL & Open Meetings Law

    Freedom of Information Law requests and Open Meetings Law compliance for publicly funded institutions.

    Often for educational institutions.
  • Proposals, RFPs & grant applications

    Responses to requests for proposals and applications for federal and state grants and programs, including fiscal intermediary applications before the New York Department of Health.

Corporate Transactions & Commercial Contracts

Transactions and agreements that move a business forward, with particular depth in regulated healthcare deals.

7 areas
  • Mergers, acquisitions & joint ventures

    Structuring and negotiating acquisitions, reorganizations and joint ventures, from the initial negotiation to closing, and integration afterward.

  • Due diligence

    Evaluating a target before purchase, identifying issues early, and completing the regulatory filings a transaction requires.

  • Commercial contracts

    Drafting and negotiating contracts with vendors, third parties and business partners.

  • Restructuring & dissolution

    Reorganizations, expansion and wind-down, business closures and the employment issues that come with them.

  • Real estate

    Real estate matters connected to a client’s operations.

    Often for hospitality and manufacturing clients.
  • Franchising, licensing & distribution

    Franchising, licensing and distribution arrangements.

    Often for hospitality clients.
  • Intellectual property & trade secrets

    Trademarks, intellectual property and the protection of trade secrets and confidential information.

    Often for manufacturing and hospitality clients.
Practice area

Workplace Advisory & Risk Management

Preventive, hands-on support that keeps workplace issues from becoming claims.

7 areas
  • Policies & handbooks

    Workplace policies and employee handbooks, including policies that align leave rules across multi-state workforces.

  • Workplace investigations

    Internal investigations of workplace misconduct, including harassment, discrimination and whistleblower allegations, and coordination with government and law enforcement where needed.

  • Leave & accommodation management

    Guiding employers through the interactive process for employees with disabilities, intermittent FMLA leave and attendance documentation.

  • Independent contractors & gig workers

    Agreements and policies that support independent contractor status, and oversight of contractor relationships.

  • Management & employee training

    Training programs, including:

    • Wage and hour compliance for managers
    • Employee leave laws for administrative and executive staff
    • Responding to union organizing
    • Preventing harassment and discrimination
    • Best hiring practices
    • Protecting trade secrets and confidential information
  • Employee relations & culture

    Culture surveys and assessments, support through rapid growth or a merger, open-door and reporting channels, and advice to boards on workplace culture.

  • Outsourced HR counsel

    Day-to-day human resources support from the firm’s attorneys and consultants for organizations without a full in-house HR function.
