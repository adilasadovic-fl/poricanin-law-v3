# Founder sections and "Commitment beyond our clients" (design, approved 2026-11-21)

## Intent
Apply Emina's feedback (Nov 18): keep the attorney + business-founder idea, but as the firm's approach rather than
promotion of her other companies; the About page is about the firm, with her biography as its own section. Add the
firm's corporate citizenship (her charities and professional involvement) as a small section on About us.

## Decisions
- Homepage founder section: heading "Counsel from a lawyer who knows what it takes to run a business"; name line;
  intro "Founded by labor and employment attorney Emina Poricanin, the firm combines almost 20 years…"; three plain
  points (no markers); credentials as one sentence; buttons "Read Emina's biography" and "Meet our people";
  photo without caption. No label above the heading.
- About hero: "A law firm for employers, founded by Emina Poricanin", one paragraph from her "Who We Are" text,
  link to her biography. No label above the heading.
- About biography (#founder, after "Our firm"): four paragraphs; her payroll technology company is mentioned without a
  name; four credential boxes (Best Lawyers, PHR, J.D., Admitted to practice) replace the old eight-box band.
- About "Commitment beyond our clients" (after "Our values"): text only; Aid for Children of War (version B, no
  personal story), SVOJA, Professional organizations (NYSHCP general counsel).

## Approaches considered
- Chosen: a small section on About us. Rejected: a separate Impact page (too little content, gives the charities more
  weight than Emina wants); photo cards (taller, need images and permission).

## Left out / open
- Female ownership: left out until certification status is known (Emina mentioned "minority women owned"; no
  certification found).
- SVOJA's final web address (the English site is a draft on a preview address) and Emina's role there.
- Emina's approval of all new wording.
