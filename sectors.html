Healthcare

Healthcare providers facing constant regulatory change, consolidation and reimbursement pressure.

Find out more about our healthcare practice

Who we serve

  • Hospitals, large medical groups and academic medical centers
  • Nursing home chains and hospices
  • Ambulatory surgery and urgent care centers, and their management companies
  • Physician and dental practice management companies
  • Drug and alcohol rehabilitation providers
  • Digital health companies
  • Payors
  • Medical device, pharmaceutical and medical supply manufacturers and distributors

Typical matters

  • Formation, licensure and market entry
  • Governance and compliance programs
  • HIPAA and privacy
  • Medicare, Medicaid and reimbursement
  • Audits, investigations and appeals
  • Healthcare litigation and dispute resolution
  • Mergers, acquisitions and joint ventures
  • Labor and employment, including wage parity
  • Legislative advocacy
Sector

Home Care

Home care providers nationally, in one of the fastest-growing industries in the United States and one with one of its largest workforces.

Find out more about our home care practice

Who we serve

  • Licensed home care services agencies (LHCSAs)
  • Certified home health agencies (CHHAs)
  • Fiscal intermediaries
  • Home care industry associations

Typical matters

  • Licensing, certification and accreditation
  • Corporate compliance programs and outside compliance officer
  • Medicaid and Medicare conditions of participation
  • Wage parity and wage and hour compliance
  • Department of Health and Department of Labor audits
  • Acquisitions, sales and due diligence
  • Fiscal intermediary applications and RFPs
  • Disputes with payors and managed care organizations
  • Noncompete enforcement and whistleblower complaints

Emina has served as general counsel to the New York State Association of Health Care Providers.

  • Disability accommodations

  • Investigations of employee misconduct
  • Litigation defense
